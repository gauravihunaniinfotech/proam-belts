    Belt Of The Month

    Global Domination Championship Belt

    On Sale

    Bring world-class recognition to your champions with the Global Domination Custom White Championship Award Belt. Showcasing five solid brass plates finished in brilliant gold lacquer, this prestigious belt features a colorfully etched globe centerpiece framed by sparkling jewels embedded directly into a pristine white strap.

    When your organization wants to present a memorable honor without long waits or inflated agency costs, a custom award belt makes a commanding statement that traditional crystal trophies and desk plaques simply cannot match. Handcrafted and customized quickly at our Ohio facility, this belt offers an affordable, expedited solution for organizations needing high-impact awards in a hurry. Every title belt in our stock championship belts collection features substantial metal hardware mounted on a flexible white strap with soft comfort backing, delivering a true 4 lb presentation feel.

    Distinct Recognition Uses
    • Corporate & Sales: Global Sales Leader, Top Territory Producer, President's Club, International Honors
    • Athletics & Tournaments: Combat Sports & MMA Fight Nights, Wrestling, Golf Outing Scrambles, Tournament MVPs
    • Competitions & Celebrations: Fantasy Football Champions, Gaming & Esports Tournaments, Milestone Honors, Cook-Offs
    Features and Specifications
    • 5 solid brass plates finished in brilliant 18k gold lacquer
    • Commanding 11” x 11” center plate featuring an etched globe design
    • Integrated decorative jewels set into a heavy-duty, flexible white strap
    • Soft comfort backing with secure snap closure and official ProAmBelts tip
    • Authentic 4 lb heavyweight presentation build
    • Available Plain or Fully Customized
    Customization Details

    Stock series belts feature high-definition, precision-cut 3M vinyl graphics applied directly to the plate faces. This process delivers crisp, full-color logos, vibrant text, and photo-quality visuals that single-color metal engraving cannot match. Stock series plates do not feature recessed metal etching. For 100% deeply engraved, fully custom metal plates built entirely from scratch, explore our premier custom title belts showroom.

    Fast, Budget-Friendly Custom Awards Shipped Quick from Ohio

    Need a championship-caliber award on short notice? We specialize in quick-turnaround, cost-effective custom recognition made right here in Ohio. Plain stock belts ship in just 1 to 2 business days. Fully customized belts ship within 7 business days following proof approval, with expedited delivery options ready for last-minute events, banquets, and tournaments. Contact us today for complimentary artwork assistance and rush support.
